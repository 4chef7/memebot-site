MemeBot1

MemeBot is a private, self-hosted tool that helps its owner schedule and publish short videos to their own social media accounts, including uploading videos to the owner's own YouTube channel as YouTube Shorts.

The tool is operated by a single person for their own channel. It is not a public service and has no other users.

MemeBot uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service.
